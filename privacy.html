4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the BurnIQ service. Specifically:

  • Account management — to authenticate you, maintain your account, and enable password reset
  • Personalized calorie goals — to calculate your daily calorie and macro targets based on your body data and goals
  • Progress tracking — to display your weight trend, calorie history, streak, BMI, and achievements
  • AI Nutrition Coach — to provide personalized nutrition advice (see Section 5)
  • Food scanning — to identify food items and estimate nutrition from photos (see Section 5)
  • Push notifications — to send optional daily logging reminders (only if you enable them)
  • PDF progress reports — generated locally on your device for sharing

  • Subscription management — to verify your subscription status via RevenueCat

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell your data to any third party, and we do not use your data to train AI models.

5. AI Features & Third-Party Data Sharing Important

BurnIQ uses Groq AI to power two features. Before using these features, the app will ask for your explicit consent. You can choose not to use AI features — the rest of the app works fully without them.

5.1 AI Nutrition Coach

What data is sent:

  • Your chat messages to the coach
  • Basic profile context: age, weight, height, goals, activity level
  • Recent nutrition summary: today's calories, macros, and water intake
  • Your current streak and goal information

Who it is sent to: Groq, Inc. via their API at api.groq.com

Purpose: To generate personalized nutrition advice and responses to your questions

Data retention: Groq processes the request in real time. Data is not stored by Groq beyond the immediate API request

Groq Privacy Policy: https://groq.com/privacy-policy/

5.2 AI Food Scanner

What data is sent:

  • The food photo you capture with your camera (as a base64 image)
  • A text prompt asking Groq to identify the food and estimate nutrition

Who it is sent to: Groq, Inc. via their vision API at api.groq.com

Purpose: To identify food items in photos and estimate calories and macronutrients

Data retention: Images are processed in real time. Images are not stored by BurnIQ or Groq after analysis

Groq Privacy Policy: https://groq.com/privacy-policy/

5.3 AI Food Search Fallback

What data is sent: A food description text query (e.g. "scrambled eggs")

Who it is sent to: Groq, Inc.

Purpose: To estimate nutrition values when the food database returns no results

Data retention: Not stored beyond the immediate request

5.4 User Consent for AI Features

Before using AI features for the first time, BurnIQ displays a clear consent dialog explaining:

  • What data will be transmitted
  • Who it will be sent to (Groq AI)
  • That data is not stored after processing
  • A link to this Privacy Policy

Users must tap "I Understand — Continue" to proceed. Users who decline are not required to use AI features — all other app functionality remains available.

7. Medical Calculations & Citations

Medical Disclaimer: BurnIQ is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice. All health calculations are estimates based on validated scientific formulas. Individual results will vary. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making significant dietary, exercise, or lifestyle changes.

7.1 Calorie Estimation (BMR/TDEE)

BurnIQ estimates your daily calorie needs and calories burned using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which is the formula most widely recommended by dietitians and healthcare professionals for estimating resting metabolic rate.

Primary Citation: Mifflin MD, St Jeor ST, Hill LA, Scott BJ, Daugherty SA, Koh YO. "A new predictive equation for resting energy expenditure in healthy individuals." American Journal of Clinical Nutrition. 1990 Feb;51(2):241-7. PMID: 2305711. View on PubMed →

Activity Multipliers: Harris JA, Benedict FG. "A biometric study of human basal metabolism." Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences. 1918;4(12):370-373. Revised by Roza AM, Shizgal HM. "The Harris Benedict equation reevaluated: resting energy requirements and the body cell mass." American Journal of Clinical Nutrition. 1984;40(1):168-82. View on PubMed →

7.2 BMI Calculation

Body Mass Index (BMI) is calculated using the standard WHO formula: weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²).

Citation: World Health Organization. "Obesity: preventing and managing the global epidemic. Report of a WHO consultation." World Health Organ Tech Rep Ser. 2000;894:i-xii, 1-253. View WHO Reference →

BMI Classifications (WHO standard):

8. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored using Supabase, a SOC 2 Type II certified cloud platform hosted on AWS
  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2+
  • Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a salt factor — never stored in plain text
  • Row-level security (RLS) policies ensure each user can only access their own data
  • API keys and secrets are never stored in the app binary — they are stored server-side
  • BurnIQ undergoes regular security reviews and updates dependencies promptly

9. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is retained for as long as your account is active
  • Account deletion: When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days
  • AI processing: Chat messages and food images sent to Groq AI are not retained beyond the real-time request
  • Backups: Supabase maintains encrypted backups for disaster recovery. These are purged on a rolling 30-day schedule
  • Local cache: Some data is cached locally on your device in AsyncStorage for performance. This is cleared when you delete your account

To delete your account: Open BurnIQ → Profile → Settings → scroll to bottom → tap "Delete Account".
