Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

BurnIQ ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the BurnIQ mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account
  • Profile Data: Age, gender, height, weight, fitness goals, and activity level
  • Health & Fitness Data: Food diary entries, calorie intake, macronutrient data, and weight history
  • Usage Data: App interactions, features used, and session information
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and personalize the BurnIQ app experience
  • Calculate calorie goals, macro targets, and BMI
  • Power the AI nutrition coach with your personal context
  • Track your progress and generate reports
  • Send push notifications (only if you enable them)
  • Process subscription payments via RevenueCat and Apple
  • Improve our app and fix bugs

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely stored using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL) for all data transmission. Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Third-Party Services

BurnIQ uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Database and authentication — Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management — Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign In — Privacy Policy  |  Google Sign In — Privacy Policy
  • Groq AI — AI nutrition coach and food recognition — Privacy Policy
  • Open Food Facts — Food nutrition database — Privacy Policy  |  USDA FoodData — Privacy Policy
  • Resend — Transactional emails (password reset) — Privacy Policy

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

5. Health Data

BurnIQ collects health and fitness information including weight, calorie intake, and nutritional data. This data is used solely to provide the app's core functionality. We do not sell or share your health data with third parties for advertising purposes.

BurnIQ is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical decisions.

6. AI Features & Data Transmission

BurnIQ uses artificial intelligence powered by Groq AI (groq.com) for the following features:

  • AI Nutrition Coach: Your chat messages and basic profile context (age, weight, height, goals, and recent nutrition data) are transmitted to Groq's API to generate personalized nutrition responses. This data is processed in real time and is not stored by Groq beyond the immediate request.
  • AI Food Scanner: Food images captured by your camera are transmitted to Groq's vision API for food identification and nutrition estimation. Images are processed in real time and are not stored by Groq.
  • Food Search Fallback: When food search returns no results, a food description is sent to Groq's API to estimate nutrition values.

Important: We do not use your conversations, images, or personal data to train AI models. Data sent to Groq AI is subject to Groq's Privacy Policy. No personal health data is permanently stored by third-party AI providers.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app's Profile → Settings → Delete Account option. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

BurnIQ is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time through the app
  • Correct inaccurate data in your profile settings
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your progress data (via the share/export feature)
  • Opt out of push notifications in your device settings

10. Cookies & Tracking

BurnIQ does not use cookies or cross-app tracking. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of BurnIQ after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
